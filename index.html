D.O.T Imports

Your Ride, Your Way

D.O.T Imports is a Bahamas-based vehicle sourcing and importation company dedicated to making vehicle ownership more accessible, affordable, and convenient. We specialize in helping customers source and import vehicles that meet their individual preferences, needs, and budgets. Through auction-based sourcing and personalized purchasing assistance, we provide customers with access to a wide selection of vehicles from international markets. Our services include vehicle sourcing, auction bidding assistance, purchase coordination, international shipping arrangements, customs brokerage and clearance, and vehicle delivery coordination. At D.O.T Imports, we believe purchasing a vehicle should be a straightforward and transparent experience. We prioritize clear communication, customer involvement, and informed decision-making throughout the importation process. From selecting the right vehicle to coordinating its arrival in The Bahamas, our goal is to simplify the process while helping customers make confident purchasing decisions.


  • Our Mission
    To provide reliable, transparent, and customer-focused vehicle importation services that make purchasing quality vehicles from international markets easier and more accessible.

  • Our Vision
    To become a trusted and recognizable name in vehicle importation throughout The Bahamas, known for professionalism, customer satisfaction, and dependable service.

  • Our Commitment
    Your preferences drive our process. Your budget guides our recommendations. Your approval determines your purchase.
